Get started

What you will need

Your xFi Gateway ships to your door, and the Xfinity app guides you through every step. Have your phone, the Gateway, and the included cables handy, then work down the timeline below.

  1. 1

    About 2 minutes

    Download the Xfinity app

    Get the free Xfinity app and sign in with your account. It becomes your setup guide, WiFi dashboard, and support line all in one place.

  2. 2

    About 3 minutes

    Plug in your xFi Gateway

    Connect the Gateway to your cable outlet and power. The lights will blink while it wakes up, and the app shows you exactly which cable goes where.

  3. 3

    About 5 minutes

    Activate your service

    Tap activate in the app. It connects your Gateway to the Xfinity network and turns on your gig-speed WiFi, usually in just a few minutes.

  4. 4

    About 2 minutes

    Name your WiFi and connect

    Create a WiFi name and password, then connect your phone, TV, and everything else. Whole-home coverage kicks in right away.

  5. 5

    Ongoing

    Manage it all from the app

    Turn on Advanced Security, set parental controls, and check your speeds anytime. Your rate is locked with the 5-year price guarantee, so there are no surprises later.

That is it. You are online.
