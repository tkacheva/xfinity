Does Xfinity Mobile include Care Plus screen repairs?

Yes — as part of Mobile Plus. Xfinity Mobile Care Plus is bundled into every Mobile Plus line at no added monthly cost and covers unlimited $0 screen repair and eligible back-glass repair, $0 coverage for mechanical breakdowns, same-day in-store battery replacement, and accidental damage, loss, and theft protection with claims up to $5,000 per device per year. It applies to eligible smartphones, tablets, and smartwatches connected to the account, regardless of where the device was purchased. Mobile Select doesn't include Care Plus, but device protection can be added starting around $9–19/mo per device, capped at 3 claims per 12 months.

Does Xfinity Mobile support multi-device plans?

Yes. An account can carry up to 12 lines, mixing smartphones, tablets, smartwatches, and data-only devices. Pricing is flat per line — $30/mo for Mobile Select or $45/mo for Mobile Plus — rather than a stacked discount for adding more lines, though promotional offers (like a free line for the first year) can offset the cost of adding devices. On Mobile Plus, Care Plus device protection also covers eligible tablets and watches on the account, not just phones, so a multi-device household gets the same repair and replacement coverage across the board.

Does Xfinity Mobile offer signal boosters?

No — Xfinity Mobile doesn't sell or include a cellular signal booster. What it offers instead is WiFi PowerBoost, a feature that automatically increases speeds up to a gigabit when a phone connects to a compatible Xfinity Gateway at home, or to one of Xfinity's 20+ million secure WiFi hotspots away from home. It's a WiFi speed enhancement, not a device or add-on that amplifies cellular signal strength — so it won't help in areas with weak cell coverage, only where WiFi is available.

Does Xfinity Mobile include hotspot access?

Yes, on both plans. Mobile Select includes mobile hotspot data with reduced speeds after 15GB of use; Mobile Plus includes unlimited hotspot data with reduced speeds after 50GB. Both plans also include access to Xfinity's network of 20+ million secure WiFi hotspots across the country, and that connection benefits from WiFi PowerBoost automatically speeding it up when a compatible device is nearby.

Does Xfinity Mobile offer cloud storage?

No — cloud storage isn't a Mobile plan perk. Xfinity's cloud backup offering (Secure Backup & Share) is tied to Xfinity Internet service, not to Mobile Select or Mobile Plus. Anyone who wants automatic photo or file backup on a Mobile line will need to add it through Xfinity Internet or use a separate cloud storage service — it isn't bundled in.

Does Xfinity Mobile include travel benefits?

Yes, on both plans. Xfinity Mobile lets customers use their unlimited talk, text, and data the same way in Canada and Mexico as they would at home, at no extra charge. For travel further afield, both Mobile Select and Mobile Plus include international roaming — talk, text, and data — in 215+ destinations worldwide, so a line stays connected without a separate international add-on in most cases.

Frequently asked questions
Is Xfinity Mobile Care Plus the same as Care (without Plus)?
No. Standard Mobile Care is a paid add-on ($9–19/mo per device) with a 3-claim annual limit, available on either plan. Care Plus is the version bundled free into Mobile Plus, with unlimited claims and no added monthly fee.
Does Xfinity Mobile require Xfinity Internet?
No, Xfinity Mobile can be purchased on its own. However, having Xfinity Internet unlocks additional promotional pricing, such as a free Mobile line for the first year.
Can hotspot data be used abroad?
International roaming covers talk, text, and data in 215+ destinations; hotspot behavior while roaming can differ from domestic use, so it's worth checking current terms for a specific destination before traveling.
