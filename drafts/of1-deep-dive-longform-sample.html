Fast WiFi, minus the jargon

Internet shopping is full of acronyms and asterisks. This guide skips all that. We will walk through what gig speed means for your home, what the xFi Gateway does, how same-day self-install works, and why your price stays put for five years.

Gig-speed fiber, built for a busy home

Gig means up to 1 gigabit per second of download speed, powered by fiber. In everyday terms, that is enough headroom to stream in 4K, jump on video calls, and game online across dozens of devices at the same time, without anyone buffering.

The xFi Gateway does the heavy lifting

The xFi Gateway is your modem and router in one box, included with your plan. It spreads whole-home WiFi to every room and unlocks Advanced Security and parental controls right inside the Xfinity app, so you manage the whole network from your phone.

Online the day you move in

Most Xfinity Internet plans support same-day self-install. Your equipment ships to you, the Xfinity app walks you through each step, and you are usually online in minutes, with no technician visit to schedule and no waiting around.

One price, locked for five years

The 5-year price guarantee locks your base monthly rate for 60 months. There is no annual contract, so you keep the freedom to change or cancel anytime. Even if you move within our service area, your rate comes with you.

Ready when you are
